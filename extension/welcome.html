Scouter Browser Defense

Stops "paste-this-command" scams and phishing — right in your browser.

You're protected. It's already running on every page.

There's nothing to set up. The guard watches quietly in the background and only speaks up when something looks dangerous. The toolbar icon shows how many threats it has caught.

🛑

ClickFix scams

If a page copies a command to your clipboard and tells you to paste it into Terminal, PowerShell, or the Run box to "verify you are human," you get a big red warning — before you can paste it — plus a one-click "Clear my clipboard."

🪤

Fake "verify you are human" pages

A banner flags the fake-CAPTCHA lure pages these scams use, so you know not to follow their instructions.

🎣

Phishing sign-ins

A banner warns you when a page asks for a password on an untrustworthy web address — a look-alike domain, a raw IP, or an insecure connection.

It works on its own — and stays private

  • No account, no sign-up, nothing to configure.
  • Everything happens on your device. On its own, it makes no network requests at all.
  • It never reads the things you copy — it only checks what a web page puts on your clipboard, and never stores or sends it.

Optional: pair with your Scouter agent

If you run the free, open-source Scouter agent on this computer, you can pair this extension with it (over your own computer only) to check a destination against live malware blocklists and block a malicious site across your whole device — reversibly — with one click. It stays fully standalone if you don't.
