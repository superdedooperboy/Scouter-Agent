## How to sign

You do not need to send anything separately. When you open your first pull request, an
automated check will ask you to confirm, and your confirmation is recorded against your
GitHub account for subsequent contributions.

Comment on the pull request with exactly:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

**Contributing on behalf of a company?** Say so on the pull request before signing. Employer
IP clauses routinely cover side projects, and a corporate CLA signed by someone authorised
to bind the company is the clean way to handle it — for your protection as much as ours.
